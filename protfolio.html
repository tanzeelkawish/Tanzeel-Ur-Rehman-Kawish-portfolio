About Me

I am a Pharmacist (Doctor of Pharmacy) with diverse practical experience across hospital, community, and industrial pharmacy settings. My professional expertise includes clinical pharmacy practice, medication dispensing, prescription review, patient counselling, inventory management, pharmaceutical production, and Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP) compliance.

Beyond traditional pharmacy practice, I have developed a keen interest in clinical research, medical documentation, and healthcare data management, including Electronic Medical or Health Records (EMR/EHR). My involvement in medical data annotation, AI-based medical applications, and the evaluation of healthcare documentation demonstrates my commitment to exploring the evolving role of artificial intelligence and digital health in transforming modern medical practice.

My broader professional interests include clinical practice, clinical research, medical data expertise, digital health and telehealth, and the responsible integration of AI innovation into healthcare. My objective is to contribute to healthcare organisations by combining pharmaceutical knowledge, practical experience, research capabilities, and emerging digital health skills to promote medication safety, quality assurance, efficient healthcare services, and continuous professional development.
